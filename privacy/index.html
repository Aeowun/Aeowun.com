PRIVACY POLICY

This page describes the main privacy considerations for the AEOWUN website and software.

LAST UPDATED // SEPTEMBER 2026

THE WEBSITE

The AEOWUN website may process information normally involved in delivering a website, including information provided directly by visitors and technical information handled by hosting, security, analytics, or advertising systems.

ADVERTISING

AEOWUN may display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information according to their own policies. Advertising is separate from the operation of AeoEngine and AeoScript.

CONTACT

If you contact AEOWUN by email or through a support form, the information you provide is used to respond to your request, investigate the issue, and maintain the support record needed to handle it.

SOFTWARE

Privacy practices can differ between AEOWUN software. Product-specific behavior should be considered separately from the website. AeoEngine and AeoScript documentation will identify external services or data flows that are part of the software when those systems become applicable.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Links and integrations can take you to services operated by other organizations. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies rather than this page.

QUESTIONS

Questions about this page or AEOWUN privacy practices can be sent to support@aeowun.com.
