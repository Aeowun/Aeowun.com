PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

AEOWUN ("AEOWUN," "we," "us," or "our") develops and distributes local-first Windows software, including AEOPIN and CheckMaker. This Privacy Policy explains how these applications handle information when you use them.

1. INFORMATION THE APPLICATIONS HANDLE

AEOPIN and CheckMaker are designed to operate on your Windows computer and work with files and other information that you choose to provide to them.

Depending on how you use the applications, they may handle information such as:

  • Files and folders that you explicitly open, provide, capture, or save through the application.
  • File names, paths, file types, timestamps, and other filesystem metadata associated with those files.
  • Content that AEOPIN processes or indexes for local search, organization, and retrieval.
  • Markdown document content that CheckMaker reads in order to display documents and locate checkbox markers.
  • Information you enter directly into the applications, such as search queries, notes, or file paths.

2. LOCAL PROCESSING

AEOPIN and CheckMaker are local-first Windows applications. Their core workflows run on the computer where the applications are installed.

In the current releases, AEOPIN and CheckMaker do not transmit the files, folders, Markdown documents, notes, or other application data you provide to AEOWUN-operated external servers as part of their normal core workflows.

3. AEOPIN

AEOPIN is a local capture and retrieval application for files, folders, links, and notes.

Captured items and the local application data required to organize, search, and retrieve them are stored on the computer running AEOPIN.

AEOPIN does not require an AEOWUN account for its core functionality.

4. CHECKMAKER

CheckMaker is a standalone Markdown checklist editor. Markdown files opened in CheckMaker remain on the local computer.

CheckMaker reads Markdown content locally for display and handles checkbox changes locally. Its editing workflow is designed to modify the relevant checkbox marker rather than send the document to a remote service.

The published CheckMaker Windows build does not require an AEOWUN account or cloud service for its core editing functions.

5. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

AEOPIN and CheckMaker do not require an AEOWUN account for their core functionality.

You are not required to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or similar account information simply to use these applications.

6. ADVERTISEMENTS IN THE APPLICATIONS

AEOPIN and CheckMaker do not display advertisements within the applications.

Advertising displayed on the AEOWUN website is separate from the applications and is not part of AEOPIN or CheckMaker.

7. ANALYTICS AND TELEMETRY

The current AEOPIN and CheckMaker releases do not send analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking information to AEOWUN-operated servers as part of the applications' normal operation.

This statement describes the current application releases. If an application's data-collection behavior changes, this policy will be updated.

8. DATA STORED ON YOUR COMPUTER

AEOPIN and CheckMaker may create local files and application state required for their operation. Depending on the application and features used, this can include configuration, indexes, recent-file information, cached state, or other local application data.

These files remain on the computer where the application is running unless you or another process on that computer moves, deletes, copies, or otherwise accesses them.

9. WEBSITE ADVERTISING

The AEOWUN website may display advertisements supplied by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Website advertising is separate from AEOPIN and CheckMaker. This application privacy policy does not describe the data practices of third-party advertising providers when you visit the AEOWUN website.

Information about website privacy and third-party services should be considered separately from the local application behavior described above.

10. THIS POLICY AND FUTURE RELEASES

This policy describes the current AEOPIN and CheckMaker releases. AEOWUN may change application functionality over time.

Material changes to how these applications handle information will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

11. CONTACT

Privacy questions about AEOPIN or CheckMaker may be submitted through the AEOWUN support page .
