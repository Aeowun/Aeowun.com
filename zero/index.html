ZERO

Research into an intelligence layer that understands architecture, constraints, and behavior—not just text.

THE HYPOTHESIS

REASONING NEEDS STRUCTURE.

Zero explores the semantic layer between neural reasoning and deterministic engineering systems. The model may generalize, but the runtime still needs explicit invariants and direct evidence.

ARCHITECTURE

UNDERSTAND SYSTEMS

Model relationships and structural invariants rather than treating a project as a flat text prompt.

CONSTRAINTS

KNOW WHAT MUST HOLD

Separate required behavior from plausible suggestions and make boundaries visible to the runtime.

RESEARCH

PREDICT SIDE EFFECTS

Explore how intelligent systems can reason about state changes before an action is authorized.

STATUS

RESEARCH ONLY.

Zero is not a downloadable product or a hosted model. It is a research direction that informs Aeowun’s engineering tools, including Magy and future runtimes.
