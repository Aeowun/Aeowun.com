AEIN

A local operator that executes filesystem and process tasks through explicit policies, observation, and verification.

THE IDEA

ACTION SHOULD BE AUDITABLE.

AEIN explores how a local agent can act on a machine without turning model output into unchecked authority. The runtime owns policy boundaries, process controls, and evidence of what happened.

POLICY

BOUNDED CAPABILITIES

Filesystem roots and process actions are constrained by runtime policy rather than a model’s preference.

OBSERVE

REPORT REALITY

Actions return machine feedback so the operator can distinguish intent from what actually occurred.

EXPERIMENTAL

NOT A GENERAL ASSISTANT

AEIN is an active research project, not a finished autonomous computer replacement or hosted service.

STATUS

EXPERIMENTAL // LOCAL

AEIN is being developed as a local runtime and operator console. Availability, supported platforms, and future service boundaries may change as the safety model develops.
