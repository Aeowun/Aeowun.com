SOUND & AUDIO

Play sound effects when doors open, traps trigger, or coins are collected using AudioEmitters.

LESSON 10

1. WHAT YOU'RE MAKING

Sound effects make your 3D world feel alive. In AeoEngine, you place an `AudioEmitter` block in your level and control its playback from scripts when game events happen!

CODE

2. WORKING EXAMPLE

AEOFILE ENVIRONMENT_SOUNDS.AEO 
// Find the sound object placed in your level
const door_sound = find("DoorSound")[0]

// Start from a known stopped state
door_sound.sound.stop()

// Play the sound whenever the door_open event arrives
on door_open() {
  door_sound.sound.play()
  wait(1)
  door_sound.sound.stop()
}
BREAKDOWN

3. LINE-BY-LINE BREAKDOWN

`door_sound.sound`: Accesses the audio handle on the `AudioEmitter` block.

`door_sound.sound.play()`: Triggers sound playback immediately through AeoEngine's audio system.

`door_sound.sound.stop()`: Stops the audio effect after 1 second.

TRY IT YOURSELF

4. TRY CHANGING SOMETHING

Try adjusting the sound volume before playing: `door_sound.sound.volume = 0.5`. Walk near the door in AeoEngine to hear the quieter sound effect!

5. COMMON BEGINNER MISTAKE

Calling a method on the Cell instead of its Sound: door_sound is the Cell handle. Call door_sound.sound.play() after retrieving its Sound handle. AeoScript also supports colon method syntax where a method expects its receiver.

Unassigned Audio Path: Make sure your `AudioEmitter` block in the Editor has an audio file assigned in its Properties panel, or `play()` won't hear anything!
