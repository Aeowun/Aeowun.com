GAMEPLAY CAMERA

Choose between the implemented third-person and first-person gameplay camera modes.

AEOENGINE // FUNDAMENTALS

CORE CONCEPT

AeoEngine keeps the editor camera separate from the gameplay camera. In Play mode, projects can use a third-person camera that follows and orbits the character with voxel collision handling, or a first-person camera that follows the character with mouse-look.

WORKFLOW / INTERACTION

BEHAVIOR PROFILE

The selected gameplay camera runs separately from the editor camera. Its behavior depends on the camera mode selected for the project:

CAMERA BEHAVIOR PROFILE 
Camera behavior

- Editor: a flying camera; its state is saved before Play and restored after.
- Third-person: follows and orbits the character; resolves collisions with voxels.
- First-person: follows the character and uses mouse-look.

EXPLANATION: Because the editor and gameplay cameras are separate, testing a World does not overwrite the editor view you were using to build it.

COMMON NOTES / LIMITATIONS

Third-Person Only: Voxel collision handling applies to the third-person follow/orbit view.

State Preservation: When stopping simulation, control restores to the editor camera exactly where it sat prior to activating Play mode.

Project Profiles: Projects can contain camera profiles in their cameras directory; World settings select the active profile.
